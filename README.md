## Gokaran Shukla

Postdoctoral Research Fellow at **KAUST**, working in computational condensed-matter physics and materials science:
spin–orbit and orbital torques, orbitronics, magnetism, strongly correlated electrons, spin-dependent transport
and charge transport in organic semiconductors and devices, from first principles (DFT, Wannier functions,
Green's functions) to kinetic Monte Carlo and device models.
